voiceannotate transcribes MP3 and WAV recordings and tells the voices apart: the text is cut into passages, each attributed to a speaker. It works entirely offline; nothing leaves this Mac.

This installer places voiceannotate in your Applications folder, together with everything it needs:

  • the speech recognition engine (Vosk),
  • the runtime for its interface (Tcl/Tk),
  • where included in this build, ready-to-use recognition models. Models for other languages can be downloaded from within the application (Download a model…).

The application runs natively on Apple silicon and on Intel Macs, and needs macOS 11 or later.
